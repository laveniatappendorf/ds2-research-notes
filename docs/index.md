# Dark Souls II research notes

Unofficial working notes from taking Dark Souls II apart: the 2013 PlayStation 3 Network Test, the retail discs,
the patches, and the PC *Scholar of the First Sin* build. They cover what the files contain, how some mechanics work,
and what was cut along the way.

These are **research notes, not a player guide**. They are long, detailed and written for people who like reading data.
If you want practical advice for playing the game, the community wikis are the better place to start.

!!! warning "Please read this first"
    Not everything here has been checked twice. Every page carries a [status label](status-labels.md) that says how
    far it can be trusted. Treat anything labelled *Not yet rated* as a lead, not a fact, and check it yourself
    before you repeat it.

## Using these notes

- Use the search box (top of the page) to find an item, enemy or id.
- Pages are dated where the work was dated. A finding may have been overtaken by later work.
- If something is wrong or you can confirm or disprove a claim, please say so by opening an issue. Corrections are the
  most useful thing anyone can send.

See [how to read this site](how-to-read.md) for the naming used (build codes, "NT", "retail"), and [about](about.md)
for how the notes were made.
